\section{Steelhead Life Cycle}
\label{sec:life-cycle}
Steelhead life histories vary in freshwater and ocean residence, migration and maturation timing, age, and repeat spawning. These patterns reflect both genetic adaptation and flexible responses to environmental conditions, forming a continuum rather than a single fixed sequence \parencite[pp.~791--792]{myers2018steelhead}. The table groups this diversity into practical stages for comparing mortality risks across environments. It represents the principal anadromous spawning pathway; individual fish and populations may follow alternative pathways or cross stage boundaries at different times. Mortality Range is left blank for later assessment.

Sources and interpretation: This table synthesizes life-history stage definitions from \textcite{blanton2011foundations}, \textcite{nmfsodfw2011uwr}, \textcite{nmfs2019puget}, \textcite{noaaGlossary}, and \textcite{myers2018steelhead}. Stage groupings, renamed categories, the early marine endpoint, and environment assignments are adaptations for this analysis, not a standardized NOAA classification.

Environment boundaries are schematic and intentionally independent of life-stage boundaries. Merged cells show continuous habitat use; divisions within a stage show transitions among freshwater, estuary (where present), and marine waters. Their vertical positions do not represent time spent in each habitat or precise biological thresholds. Smolt and early marine terminology overlaps during initial marine migration.

Alternative pathways: The table represents the principal anadromous spawning pathway, not an obligatory sequence for every fish. Half-pounders may return to freshwater after their first ocean summer, overwinter without spawning, and re-enter the ocean. Other pathways include lagoon rearing, freshwater residency and interbreeding with resident Rainbow Trout, prolonged freshwater holding after spawning, and consecutive or skip spawning. Post-spawning ocean recovery is therefore not required between every spawning event \parencite[pp.~781--782, 792, 798, 820, 852--855]{myers2018steelhead}.
