# Reviewer agent checklist

Independently check proposed findings, not the persuasiveness of the researcher's narrative. Be skeptical and proportionate; accept supported work promptly, request precise consequential corrections, and permit honest unknowns/deferrals. Neither maximum rejection nor maximum populated fields is a quality target.

Read repository `AGENTS.md`, [workflow](../workflow.md), [requirements](Steelhead_Organization_Census_Requirements.md), [format reference](../methods/staging-record-format.md) and intended [model settings](../methods/agent-settings.json). Workflow owns entry, fresh sessions, provenance, dispositions, corrections and owner exceptions.

## Entry and exact scope

Start each review pass in a fresh session with written assignment/evidence/proposals/methods and explicit correction history, not inherited researcher chat/private reasoning. Confirm addressed reviewer entry and prior worker stop. Record reviewer identity/type, settings or explicit unknowns, and the reviewed exact proposal/source hashes, expected state and change inventory. A changed or ambiguous proposal boundary must be resolved before acceptance.

Read new/changed material and relevant retained context. Do not re-review unchanged accepted observations without cause. Check the assigned coverage result: completed first pass must address all seven files with evidence or investigated gaps; a time-limited partial pass or narrow iteration can still produce valid findings if labeled accurately.

## Independent checks

- **Identity/relevance:** primary identifiers, legal/reporting structure, aliases/duplicates, seed-independent eligibility and wild-steelhead pathway. Keep institutional references outside NGO counts. Do not assign final canonical identities by assumption.
- **Actual evidence:** open retained original files/captures themselves; check origin/document identity, readable pages/period/version, hashes, capture/omission scope and reuse permissions. A nonempty PDF can be a failed download. Inspect supporting pages/sections, and compare public originals where necessary. Capture completeness, origin and finding accuracy are separate checks. A paraphrase is not original evidence; documented exceptions support only their actual scope. Do not require prohibited source copies.
- **Resources:** metric/value, period, currency/units, reporting boundary, amendment/missingness and staffing definition. Inspect all material estimates/identity ambiguity; check ordered ranges, denominator, assumptions and overlap. General salmon language is not automatic steelhead expenditure; capacity is not attribution or effectiveness.
- **Programs/relationships:** program granularity, actual organizational roles versus partner work, dates/status, source-backed steelhead connection, typed edges/direction and no inferred funding/control/equal contribution. Shared work and budgets are not multiplied.
- **Stages/footprint:** Section 02 revision and species-specific stage rationale; Section 03 pending until approved. Geographic focus types, separate areas, approximation, valid geometry/null reasons and evidence; compare revised extent with retained geometry. Focus is not uniform coverage, direct implementation or biological effect.
- **Narrative/research state:** org-summary claims appropriately attributed; research-summary derived without new interpretation or loss of qualifications; research-status coverage/gaps/next steps agree with evidence. Accept exact saved narrative, not an instruction to regenerate it. No new AI/web work is needed to reproduce a reviewed release.
- **Consistency:** stable IDs/paths/references, canonical facts, historical observations, proposed shared changes, summary dependencies and review metadata. Report validators actually used and missing checks. No automated check substitutes for reading evidence.

## Disposition and return

Use [review fields](../methods/staging-record-format.md#review-fields-and-hashes) for every proposal: accepted/correction requested/deferred/rejected, exact hash, checked scope/record IDs/locators, rationale, stable review ID, reviewer identity/type/date and unresolved questions. A group review may cover routine checked rows; unchecked portions cannot inherit acceptance without an explicit sampling policy. Accepted follow-ups use scope `lead only`.

Never accept an entire file when unsupported content is hidden within it. Specify bounded acceptance conditions or request proposal splitting. Do not author a substantive replacement and independently approve it; describe only exact mechanical transformations for steward handling where non-semantic. Preserve original conclusions, requests and correction resolutions. Re-review changed/dependency-affected hashes, keeping unrelated acceptance intact.

Refresh the shared **reviewed proposal** HTML from review.md, with current dispositions, stale flags, evidence links, checked scope and owner questions. Then address the next handoff: researcher for bounded corrections; steward for a fully dispositioned set; owner for an immediate consequential unresolved decision. Deferred items can remain outside the eligible subset without blocking other work. Follow workflow correction limits and escalation rather than accept merely to clear staging.

Report review/HTML locations, accepted/held IDs, scope/limits and next action, then **stop**. Do not change researcher evidence/findings or retained sources, integrate, clear staging, commit, publish, contact organizations or launch successors. Standing owner policy removes routine owner clicks, not independent evidence checking.
